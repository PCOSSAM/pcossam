FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? Check the answers below first.

What do you learn in generative AI training?▾
You learn to use ChatGPT and Claude the way your workflow runs. Practice is centered on ready-to-use deliverables: report writing, research, presentation planning, and organizing meeting materials.
Do you also offer AI report and proposal writing training?▾
Yes. You can practice research, outlining, key summaries, organizing tables and sentences, report drafts, and proposal writing. Training can be tailored to your organization’s actual report and proposal formats.
What is the AI marketing website building course?▾
You organize your products, customers, FAQs, and search keywords with ChatGPT, then build an AEO/GEO-structured website yourself with Claude vibe coding. Building the site isn’t the goal — it’s how you lay the foundation for AI marketing.
How are AEO and GEO handled in this course?▾
AEO and GEO aren’t a separate course but structural design criteria for building an AI marketing website. FAQs, service descriptions, structured data, and content flow are shaped so AI can read them easily.
Are corporate workshops available?▾
Yes. For company employees, generative AI training, ChatGPT-assisted slide creation, AI report/proposal writing, and the AI marketing website course can be arranged as a 2-hour lecture, half-day, full-day, or multi-session program.
Can I join even if I don’t know AI tools?▾
Yes. Explanations are kept simple enough for complete beginners, and you practice only the features you actually need for real presentation preparation.
How much does a corporate AI lecture cost?▾
An AI lecture (60-90 min) starts at 1.2M KRW, a workshop (2-4 hours) at 1.5M KRW, a 1-day intensive (6-8 hours) at 2.5M KRW, and a 2-day advanced course at 5M KRW. Programs are customized around ChatGPT usage, generative AI in practice, and AI-assisted report/proposal writing.
How much does speech coaching cost?▾
Individual coaching is 300K KRW for one 2-hour session, 800K KRW for a 3-session package, and 1.2M KRW for a 5-session package. Executive coaching starts at 1M KRW, CEO presentation prep at 3M KRW, and IR rehearsal at 2M KRW.
How much does pitch deck / proposal consulting cost?▾
By length: from 1.5M KRW for up to 10 pages, 2.5M KRW for up to 20 pages, and 3.5M KRW for 30+ pages. Investor IR decks start at 3M KRW, and large-scale proposals are quoted separately.
How is pricing determined?▾
It depends on lecture length, number of participants, consulting scope, slide volume, and number of coaching sessions. After you inquire, we send a custom quote, typically within 1-2 business days.
Do you coach English presentations?▾
Rather than specialized English pronunciation coaching, we focus on helping non-native experts prepare English presentations with clearer structure and messaging — coaching script structure, key sentences, and Q&A handling.
