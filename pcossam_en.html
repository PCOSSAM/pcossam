FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? Check here first.

Is online coaching available?▾
Yes. Using Zoom or Google Meet, we can handle slide review, script feedback, rehearsal coaching, and Q&A prep entirely online.
Can I apply without any presentation materials yet?▾
Yes. Even with just an idea or topic, we can define your purpose, audience, and key message together and design the structure. We can even draft slides on the spot with AI.
Can you improve an existing deck?▾
Yes. We can improve the message structure, slide flow, and delivery script of an existing PPT, pitch deck, sales deck, proposal, or course material.
How many sessions does speech coaching take?▾
It can be structured as a single diagnostic session, a 3-session intensive package, pre-presentation rehearsal coaching, or a full package. The integrated package averages 6 sessions (about 2 hours each) at 5,000,000 KRW.
Do you run corporate workshops?▾
Yes. For company employees, we offer AI presentation strategy, ChatGPT-based slide creation, proposal/report writing, and speech coaching workshops, structured as a 2-hour lecture, half-day, full-day, or multi-session format.
Do I need prior AI tool experience?▾
Not at all. We explain things simply enough for complete beginners, and only practice the features you actually need for your presentation.
How much does a corporate AI lecture cost?▾
An AI lecture (60-90 min) starts at 1,200,000 KRW, a workshop (2-4 hours) starts at 1,500,000 KRW, a 1-day intensive session (6-8 hours) starts at 2,500,000 KRW, and a 2-day deep-dive starts at 5,000,000 KRW. Programs are customized for your company around ChatGPT usage, generative AI in practice, and AI-assisted report/proposal writing.
How much does speech coaching cost?▾
Individual coaching is 300,000 KRW for one 2-hour session, 800,000 KRW for a 3-session package, and 1,200,000 KRW for a 5-session package. Executive coaching starts at 1,000,000 KRW, CEO presentation prep starts at 3,000,000 KRW, and IR rehearsal starts at 2,000,000 KRW.
How much does pitch deck / proposal consulting cost?▾
Based on length: 1,500,000 KRW for up to 10 pages, 2,500,000 KRW for up to 20 pages, and 3,500,000 KRW for 30+ pages. IR decks start at 3,000,000 KRW, and large-scale proposals are quoted separately.
How is pricing determined?▾
It depends on session length, number of participants, consulting scope, slide volume, and number of coaching sessions. We provide a custom quote after your inquiry, replying within 1-2 business days.
Do you coach presentations in English?▾
Rather than pronunciation coaching, we focus on helping non-native speakers prepare an English presentation with a clearer structure and message, coaching script structure, key phrasing, and Q&A handling.
