The Stroller-Parking Revolution

A small trick for not hauling an empty stroller back and forth

PCOSSAM (Mingu Kim) · AI Presentation Consultant & Coach

You always put your kid in the stroller for the walk to daycare. Sure, the kid can walk. But there are crosswalks along the way, streets with traffic. You never know which way they'll dart off, so you end up putting them in the stroller anyway.

The problem comes after. Once you drop your kid off at daycare, you have to push the empty stroller all the way back home. That's genuinely annoying. Five hours later at pickup, you have to push that empty stroller all the way back out again. Round trip. Twice. Honestly, it's less about being tired and more about it just being a hassle. I bet a lot of people know that feeling.

Then one day at pickup, I casually asked the teacher, "would it be okay if I just parked the stroller here?" "Sure, that's fine." That one sentence made me happier than it probably should have.

Remember buying the stroller in the first place? Digging through mom forums, comparing which one was good, which one was worth the money, which one was easy to wash — comparing and comparing again. All that effort you put into picking it. And yet, looking back, it just became a thing that gets used. Maybe that's exactly how it should be, come to think of it.

At some point I started going to and from daycare empty-handed. I had no idea it would feel this freeing.

Sounds like nothing, but I think this matters. Instead of quietly putting up with something inconvenient, just asking once. That one bit of nerve changes more than you'd expect.
