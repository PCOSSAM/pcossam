What is AX?

AX (AI Transformation) is an organizational change that uses AI to cut repetitive work and turns the time saved into new revenue. Most companies see AI only as an automation and cost-cutting tool. But if a 3-hour task now takes 1 hour, the real question is: where will the other 2 hours go?
