CASE 06 · Public Administration × AI

Real-Time Public-Institution Corporate Card Alerts

An AI/digital-government concept that texts citizens public-institution corporate-card transactions the instant they happen, achieving both budget transparency and fraud prevention.

PCOSSAM (Mingu Kim) · AI Presentation Consultant & Coach · Business-Planning Case Study
CHALLENGE The Problem We Set Out to Solve

"What did you just buy? Why'd you buy that, it's pointless" — when a family card is used, the alert lands instantly, and suddenly you're asking questions and feeling a bit self-conscious about spending. What if public-institution corporate-card transactions reached citizens the same way, the instant they happened? Public budget execution is an area where information asymmetry keeps citizens from seeing what's happening in real time. We propose a system that borrows the family-card-alert model to achieve both transparency and fraud prevention at once.

#TransparentBudgetExecution #AntiCorruption #ClosingTheInformationGap #RealTimeAlerts #PublicData #ParticipatoryGovernance
SOLUTION The Limits of Budget Oversight Today → The Real-Time Alert Fix

If traditional public-budget oversight was limited by

  • Budget execution only visible after the fact, in audit reports
  • Ordinary citizens unable to access real-time spending data (information asymmetry)
  • Fraudulent or personal use staying hidden until it's caught
  • Auditing and investigation eating up significant administrative cost

With a real-time text-alert system

  • Citizens sign up for alerts on any public or affiliated institution's corporate card
  • A text goes out to subscribers the instant a payment is made
  • Citizens can reply to the text to raise concerns or flag issues directly
  • Accumulated card-usage data feeds budget analysis and policy improvement
PLAN Rollout Plan — 4 Steps
1
Sign Up for Alerts
Build a system where anyone can subscribe to alerts for any public or affiliated institution's corporate card.
2
Real-Time Alerts
Text subscribers the transaction details the instant a corporate-card payment is made.
3
Raise Concerns
Subscribers can reply to the text to raise issues and post feedback.
4
Expand & Integrate
Can extend to commute/business-trip alerts, CBDC (stablecoin + blockchain) integration, and even overseas export.
IMPACT Expected Impact
🔍Strengthens transparency in public-budget spending
🚫Prevents fraudulent or personal use before it happens
🤝Citizens verify it themselves — boosting trust in government and public institutions
⚖️Builds a culture of accountable administration
💰Cuts audit and investigation costs through citizen-driven oversight
📊Improves budget and policy using accumulated data
🏛️Strengthens participatory democratic governance
🌍A globally rare model for real-time public-spending disclosure — a digital-government export case

Just as Korea's T-money public-transit system was exported abroad, a transparent, corruption-free administrative system can also be exported as a Korean digital-government model.
