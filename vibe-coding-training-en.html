Module details

M1 · Vibe coding concepts & setup

Learn the concept of vibe coding — creating deliverables through conversation with AI alone, no coding knowledge required — and prepare your hands-on environment. Check what you need beforehand (paid ChatGPT/Claude accounts, two photos of yourself, a company profile document) and set your own target deliverable.

M2 · Building a website skeleton with Claude

Ask Claude in plain sentences for what you want, and build the basic structure of your own introduction webpage. Use your company profile and personal information as raw material to refine the copy and layout.

M3 · Finishing and deploying the website

Polish colors, images, and the mobile view to raise the finished quality, then deploy (publish live) to a real web address. Walk away with a page you can link from your business card and email the moment you finish, and review the instructor's own live site as a working example.

M4 · Adding features with Codex

Use Codex (OpenAI's coding tool) to add a contact form or simple feature to your website. Get a feel for how Claude and Codex differ, and when to reach for each.

M5 · Building a digital business card

Build a digital business card that replaces paper cards, and connect it to a QR code and link. Build alongside the instructor's own digital business card as a working example.

M6 · Building an email signature

Create a professional email signature with your name, title, contact info, and links, and apply it to a real email account. Adapt the format to match your company's standards.

M7 · Automating repetitive work

Practice automating the document writing and data organizing you repeat every day using AI. Pick a recurring task from your own job and build an automation example for it.

M8 · Applying and extending at work

Map out how to bring vibe coding into your own role (planning, sales, operations, marketing, etc.) and set an extension roadmap, so you can keep building your own deliverables after the course ends.

What to bring

  • Paid ChatGPT / Claude accounts (the AI tools used in the hands-on sessions)
  • Two photos of yourself (used in the website and digital business card)
  • A company profile document (raw material for the webpage copy and structure)
