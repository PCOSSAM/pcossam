CASE 02 · Mobility × Lodging

EV-Only Hotel

A mobility hotel that combines EV charging with lodging, solving the discomfort of car camping and proposing untact, smart operations.

PCOSSAM (Mingu Kim) · AI Presentation Consultant & Coach · Business-Planning Case Study
🏢 Floor layout — Suite, Premium, Economy rooms + charging lounge
🔌 Economy room — privacy partitions + per-vehicle charging space
🌍 Global expansion concept — US, China, and Japan versions
CHALLENGE The Problem We Set Out to Solve

EVs are growing fast, but travelers still have to solve charging and lodging separately and inefficiently. Especially on weekends and during peak season, when tourist-area rooms are hard to find, many travelers end up sleeping in their cars — but without a bathroom or shower, that's uncomfortable. We propose an "EV-Only Hotel (mobility hotel)" that solves EV charging and lodging in one place.

#EVChargingInfrastructure #LodgingShortage #MixedUseRegulation #MobilityHotel #UntactSmartOperations #RegulatorySandbox
SOLUTION The Limits of EV Travel Today → The EV-Only Hotel Fix

If traditional EV travel left you dealing with

  • Booking and using charging and lodging separately
  • Hard-to-find rooms in tourist areas on weekends and during peak season
  • The discomfort of car camping with no bathroom or shower
  • Charging stations that are just functional spaces you can't stay in

With an EV-Only Hotel

  • EV-only entry — drive your car straight into the room, and charging starts the moment you park
  • Sleep in the car, plus use the building's shared bathroom, shower, and lounge
  • License-plate recognition handles booking, payment, and entry — fully untact
  • Tiered pricing by floor: Economy (₩30,000), Premium (₩50,000), Suite (₩70,000)
  • Vending machines and other amenities in the lounge
  • Converts to a regular parking lot/charging station in the off-season for operating efficiency
PLAN Rollout Plan — 4 Steps
1
Site Selection
Secure sites at tourist areas, along highways, and at key hubs.
2
Build the Facility
Fit the drive-in room structure with fire-safety systems — clearance distances, firewalls, automatic extinguishing, per-floor ventilation — and separate vehicle and pedestrian traffic.
3
Build the App Platform
Run booking, payment, and entry untact, based on license-plate recognition.
4
Pursue Formal Regulation
Use a regulatory sandbox and pilot program to establish legal standards for a 'mobility-based stay facility.'
IMPACT Expected Impact
🏓️Solves the lodging shortage in tourist areas
📈More tourists + a boosted local economy
🔌A stable revenue structure combining room fees and charging fees
🔄Converts to parking in the off-season to minimize revenue gaps
🌱Spreads an EV-based, eco-friendly lodging culture
🌍A world-first model with export potential for the Korean system
📊App-collected data feeds mid-to-long-term tourism planning
